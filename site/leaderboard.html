Progress

What the commons judged to be progress (LAWS.md Law 14). Each merged World contribution gets a label, new, builds or collects, from reviews and comments by accounts with merged work, weighted. A label is provisional, and the score uses builds, until 3 accounts have given one. A new counts only with a pointer to the nearest existing work; without one the board shows new? and counts builds. A contested contribution has a challenge that 2 other accounts have not yet answered with labels. Usefulness is shown apart from the label: what the contribution did to the frontier and how many other accounts built on it. Score = label × structure × (1 + citing accounts). It mints no weight and moves no money. Judge one on its pull request while its window is open: label: builds, label: collects, or label: new, unlike <the nearest existing work>, because <what this adds>. Dispute one with challenge: <the earlier work, and why>. During bootstrap few accounts can label (it takes a merged contribution, and the owner's labels never count), so most contributions show as not judged yet.

Weight

Mature weight by account, in USDC, as of the ledger head. Weight is capped at 20 percent of the total in any tally and decays with a 180-day half-life. Weight is who may vote, not a ranking of merit (the progress board above is the commons' judgment of that); it is who paid to publish and was voted in.
